# Split Ways car credits (cars-v2)

Copy these lines exactly into ASSETS.md, `/licenses` and the in-game credits. CC BY 4.0 needs four things: the title, the author, the source link and the licence, plus a note that the work was changed. Each line below has all four. The original titles stay as published, even where they contain a word we do not use in-game (for example "Ariel"), because the credit has to name the source correctly. Listing a title is not a trademark use, and nothing in the credit suggests the authors endorse Split Ways.

1. **Briganti Zanna**: "Fictional supercar - V12 Goblin" by Olli Teittinen (ollitei), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fictional-supercar-v12-goblin-0a20e49ad5774d778567cb5c3f345786, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Modified: converted to glTF, re-materialed, debranded (licence plate, crests, scripts, wheel-cap and tyre sidewall art removed) and renamed.

2. **Dashwood Nightjar**: "Generic Sport Coupé Car" by MMC Works (mmcworks), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/generic-sport-coupe-car-820a13c4831c41959ce75e6c2014e882, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Modified: converted to glTF, re-materialed, debranded (dashboard screen art redrawn, tyre sidewall text removed), licence plate added, textures resized and renamed.

3. **Brazen Haymaker**: "Muscle Car Concept" by ViktorKom, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/muscle-car-concept-aa5dbdc7d492478ababfd4d385b8339f, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Modified: converted to glTF, panels closed, re-materialed, debranded (licence plate, logos and badges removed), textures resized and renamed.

4. **Mudlark Scree**: "Ariel_350-V2" by orcha (gandoplyas), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ariel-350-v2-7a9a44bcf152405792424408bb573482, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Modified: converted to glTF, scaled, re-materialed and renamed.
